    Team Organization

    Product Mission Statement

    Our mission is to build an open-source autonomous robot that accurately marks construction layouts on new home foundations, making layout work faster, cheaper, and more precise than marking by hand.

    Team Charter

    Our team is committed to designing and developing an open‑source, reliable, and affordable autonomous robot that transforms how construction layout work is performed. We aim to create a system capable of accurately marking floor plans, assisting builders on new home foundations, and improving efficiency and precision in construction layout design.

    We value accessibility, transparency, and engineering excellence. By keeping our platform open-source, we empower builders, students, and innovators to adapt, improve, and expand the robot’s capabilities. Our work emphasizes safety, durability, and real‑world usability, ensuring the robot can operate effectively in demanding construction environments.

    As a team, we commit to collaboration, accountability, and continuous learning. We will communicate openly, support one another’s strengths, and uphold high standards in both technical development and project management. Our shared goal is to deliver a tool that meaningfully improves construction workflows and sets a foundation for future innovation in autonomous field robotics.

    Team Goals

    1. Improve our understanding of mechatronics.
    2. Design a project that is simple and efficient.
    3. Discuss and learn with other engineers, gaining their perspective.
    4. Gain confidence in our ability to complete a project from start to finish.
    5. Deliver a layout robot that accurately marks floor plans on new home foundations.

    Communication

    • Day to day: A team group chat.
    • Long-term coordination and records: A Discord server, where we catalog our activity so we have a shared archive of decisions and files.
    • Instructor contact: One team member emails the instructor and copies every team member.

    Meetings

    • Monday: 4:00–6:00 pm
    • Friday: 9:30–11:30 am
    • Format: Face to face, at the ASU Polytechnic dining hall or student union.
    • Reminders: The group chat. We hold each other accountable for attending.
    • Changing or adding meetings: Proposed in the group chat. If something urgent comes up, we call over Discord.

    Procedures

    • Late work: If a due date is within 3 days and your part of the assignment is not ready, you must tell the group.
    • Technical issues: We try to solve them as a team before asking the professor.
    • Interpersonal issues: We contact the professor right away.

    Conflict Recognition & Resolution Agreement

    We all collectively agree to maintain civil interaction over the course of the semester.
